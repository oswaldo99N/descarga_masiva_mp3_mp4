Nexo Descargas includes third-party software in its Windows installer.

FFmpeg and FFprobe 9.0.2, essentials build by Gyan Doshi
  The included build reports --enable-gpl and --enable-version3 and is
  distributed under GPLv3. See GPL-3.0.txt in the installation directory.
  Binary source: https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/
  FFmpeg source revision listed for this build:
  https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/commit/946fcce07b

Node.js 24.15.0
  The official Windows x64 distribution is included. Its full license and
  third-party notices are in _internal/bin/node/LICENSE.
  Source: https://nodejs.org/download/release/v24.15.0/

yt-dlp and yt-dlp-ejs
  Installed from their Python packages and bundled with the application.
  Their license metadata and notices are included under _internal.
  Sources: https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp
           https://github.com/yt-dlp/ejs

Python and PyInstaller
  The executable includes a Python runtime packaged by PyInstaller.
  Sources: https://www.python.org/
           https://pyinstaller.org/

Before publishing a new installer, review the licenses and source
