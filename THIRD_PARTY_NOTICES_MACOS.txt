Nexo Descargas for macOS includes third-party software.

FFmpeg and FFprobe 9.0.2
  Built from the official FFmpeg source with GPLv3 enabled and the LAME MP3
  encoder linked statically. The source archive is distributed alongside the
  macOS disk image. GPL-3.0.txt is included in the app bundle.
  Source: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-9.0.2.tar.gz

LAME 4.0
  The static libmp3lame library is included in the FFmpeg executables.
  Its source archive is distributed alongside the macOS disk image, and
  its license is included as lame-COPYING.txt in the app bundle.
  Source: https://downloads.sourceforge.net/project/lame/lame/4.0/lame-4.0.tar.gz

Node.js 24.15.0
  The official macOS executable is included. Its complete license and
  third-party notices are included in bin/node/LICENSE inside the app bundle.
  Source: https://nodejs.org/download/release/v24.15.0/

yt-dlp and yt-dlp-ejs
  Installed from their Python packages and bundled with the application.
  Their package metadata and notices are included inside the app bundle.
  Sources: https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp
           https://github.com/yt-dlp/ejs

Python and PyInstaller
  The app includes a Python runtime packaged by PyInstaller.
  Sources: https://www.python.org/
           https://pyinstaller.org/
